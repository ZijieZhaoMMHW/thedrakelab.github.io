We study the fluid dynamics of the ocean and how they shape the evolution of the Earth system. Our research is driven first and foremost by curiosity, but much of it is motivated by the challenge of mitigating and adapting to human-caused climate change over the coming decades to centuries. We use a hierarchy of ocean and climate models, from pen-and-paper mathematical theory to comprehensive Earth system simulations run on some of the world's largest supercomputers. We train students and postdocs to become independent researchers, generous collaborators, and leaders in ocean and climate science, preparing them for careers in academia, public service, and industry.

Our work is supported by the National Science Foundation (CAREER award, Office of Polar Programs), the
David and Lucile Packard Foundation, NASA, NOAA, the Department of Energy, and the Hellman Fellows Fund.
Henri is a 2026 Packard Fellow and a former NOAA Climate and Global Change Postdoctoral Fellow.

Ran is easy and open-minded, blending motion with stillness. Honing his knowledge and skills fuels his wandering journey so far. His research interests are broad, with past work centered on mesoscale eddies in the Southern Ocean, eddy-mean interactions, multi-scale analysis, mesoscale eddy diffusion, and climate change. He is also captivated by marine biogeochemistry and extreme events. His approach to research weaves together observational data analysis and numerical modeling. He loves running simulations, often immersing himself in them silently for days.

Chih-Lun's research interests involve stratified shear instability's role in ocean mixing. Chih-Lun is also interested in flow-topography interactions, such as island wakes and flow over ocean ridges. Using numerical simulations and theories, Chih-Lun aims to understand the complex dynamics of ocean mixing and its impact on the Earth system. Chih-Lun completed his PhD at Oregon State University, working with [Bill Smyth](https://blogs.oregonstate.edu/salty/) on Kelvin-Helmholtz Turbulence in Complex Environments.

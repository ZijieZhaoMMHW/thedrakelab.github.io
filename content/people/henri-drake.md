Henri F. Drake joined the Department of Earth System Science (ESS) at the University of California, Irvine (UCI) as an Assistant Professor in 2023. He received his B.S. in Mathematics (Honors) from Haverford College in 2015 and his PhD from the MIT/WHOI Joint Program in Oceanography in 2021. Prior to joining UCI, he was a NOAA Climate and Global Change Postdoctoral Fellow at Princeton University and NOAA's Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Laboratory. His research interests include the dynamics and kinematics of ocean turbulence, theories of large-scale ocean circulation, Antarctic ice-ocean interactions, the ocean's role in the climate system, sea level change, and numerical Earth System modeling. He is committed to making science more open, collaborative, and reproducible; broadening participation in science; and producing basic science that benefits the public good.

As the group's Principal Investigator (PI), Henri manages the group, raises funds, oversees the research and outreach programs, advises and mentors students and postdocs, and teaches relevant courses in the Earth System Science department. Outside of work, he enjoys family time, tasting local foods, hiking, running, swimming, and computer games (especially World of Warcraft: Forever).
